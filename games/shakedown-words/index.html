How to Play

Shakedown Words plays like a puzzle-board guessing game: a category and a blank phrase, and it's on you to fill it in.

  • Click letters on the keyboard (or type on your own) to call them.
  • A letter you have lands in every spot it appears in the phrase.
  • Every puzzle starts with 100 points. A correct letter is free — a wrong letter costs you 20. Run out of points and you bust: the answer's revealed and the round's a loss.
  • Once you've got a feel for it, hit Solve the phrase and type the whole thing.
  • The board fills itself in automatically once every letter's been called.
  • Stuck? Reveal answer bails you out on purpose — no shame in it, just no Balloons.
  • Toggle the category chips to draw from lot slang, song titles, band & albums, or venues & festivals.
  • Balloons are earned on every solve, based on the points you had left: solving by typing it out pays a bonus over filling in every letter, and a solve streak adds a growing bonus on top. Bust or give up and you earn none.
